# 0.7.10 — Wii texture preview and explicit root translation

The Wii sample has a diffuse image and valid UVs, but also a white emission colour. The preview previously added that colour without texture modulation, washing out the model. Imported fixed-function materials now modulate that emission by the diffuse texture when no separate emissive map is assigned. Source emission values and image pixels are retained. Reimport the Wii IGB to obtain the corrected material setup; existing materials do not automatically acquire the new import metadata.

The constant-root removal introduced in 0.7.8 is superseded. Bip01/pelvis, Motion and hierarchy-root translations remain explicit and active even when constant, preventing destination-skin rest translation from being used instead. The values come from the exported animation's evaluated scene, without a hardcoded vanilla height or a hidden import-offset correction. Missing scene Motion tracks retained from import also receive explicit constant translation from their source skeleton when appropriate. RemovePos continues to strip child-limb translation only.

Tests in Blender 4.5.3: the Wii white/washed-out result was reproduced and a texture-modulated before/after preview rendered. Both materials keep their original white emission properties and connect their diffuse images to the modulation node. The supplied Max custom animation was exported with active constant Bip01 and Motion tracks, then imported on skins with original pelvis rest height, -0.35 metres and +0.55 metres, in both skeleton-support modes. Pelvis positions match across all six variants at five sampled frames, and re-export retains the translation samples. The source scene rest heights are not rewritten by export. The 0.7.9 installation repair is retained.

These are Blender and serialized-file checks. The game itself was not run; in-game transition behaviour still requires confirmation. This release changes preview interpretation for imported fixed-function materials and root channel preservation, not arbitrary skeleton-name mapping.
