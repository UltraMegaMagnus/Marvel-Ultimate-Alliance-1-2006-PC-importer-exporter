# 0.7.14: repair joint axes without forcing a T-pose

Static Actors export now checks identifiable MUA longitudinal joint chains for gross axis mismatches. In the supplied Cage rig, each clavicle local X points about 108 degrees away from its upper-arm joint. Existing animation rotations combined with this malformed local translation send the arms across the body. The working Max and vanilla skins do not have that mismatch.

The exporter corrects the affected joint frames and rebuilds their local translations/inverse bind matrices. Mesh vertices, normals, weights, joint positions and the original Blender scene stay unchanged. Already compatible joints retain their exact authored axes and roll; no reference T-pose is baked. The root/pelvis correction from 0.7.12 remains active. Embedded model animation and standalone animation paths are unchanged.

Detection is based on reference parent/child relationships and joint geometry, not a filename or the Cage character. The reference must identify a unique child along local X; missing names, different hierarchy, zero-length or ambiguous chains are skipped. Only deviations exceeding 45 degrees trigger repair, preserving modest offsets such as the known-good Max spine. When rebuilding a mismatched frame, its orientation follows the reference frame swung toward the actual child. Pure roll mismatches and unknown skeleton conventions cannot be reliably inferred from a line between joints; this is not a guarantee for arbitrary rigs or incompatible animation naming/hierarchies.

Validation: Cage blend before/after export preserves geometry, weights, joint positions and original Blender data, changes only the two clavicle frames and corrects their animated shoulder directions. Blender renders of the supplied idle show the malformed shoulders/arms corrected. The supplied Max blend requires no repairs and preserves its 0.7.13 behavior. Packaged regression results are in the E: validation086 directory. No new in-game run was performed by the assistant; the corrected Cage test skin is provided for gameplay confirmation.

Final packaged checks passed in Blender 4.5.3: Cage/Max scene preservation, matched Max animation deformation, root-offset/repeated export, 36 synthetic axis faults on six joint chains at varied lengths, unknown/changed/zero-length hierarchy guards, idempotence, Wii/environment materials, blended embedded animation, and fresh/upgrade installation. See validation/0714_*.json.
